How to use this resource

Designed for the classroom

Each module contains a concise pre-listening vocabulary list, the authentic audio embedded in the page, and an exam-style activity. Students receive immediate, evidence-based feedback after submitting. There are no accounts, adverts, external logins or dependencies.

Recommended: pre-teach vocabulary for 3–4 minutes, allow 45–60 seconds to read the questions, play the extract twice with a short pause between listenings, then check the answers together.

For self-study, the audio player allows two full listenings before submission. Pausing does not use up an extra listening. Replaying is unlimited after submitting. Scores remain on this device only.

The pre-listening vocabulary avoids giving away the most important answers in the gap-fill task. For follow-up independent study, use the separate Housing Vocabulary Lab HTML included in the pack.
