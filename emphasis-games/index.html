Teacher's quick guide

How to play

  1. Choose a game and a case. Both students must choose the same case number.
  2. Open different roles. One uses Student A, the other uses Student B. Press Share partner's role to send their direct link.
  3. Look at your four speaking cues. Use the unfinished emphasis starters and clues to make your OWN sentences aloud. If you need a refresher, Grammar SOS explains the pattern using an unrelated example.
  4. Speak and react. Keep the speaking cues on screen. Try to use at least three structures naturally, tick them if you like, and negotiate an ending.
  5. Swap roles or try another case. Your writing is saved on your device automatically, so you can return to it later.

Good to know: There is no timer, login or automatic grammar marking. The activity is designed for live pair speaking, with the teacher giving feedback. Your optional ticks and outcomes are saved in this browser only. There is no writing involved.
