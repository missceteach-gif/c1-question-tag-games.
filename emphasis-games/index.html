Teacher's quick guide

How to play

  1. Choose a game and a case. Both students must choose the same case number.
  2. Open different roles. One uses Student A, the other uses Student B. Press Share partner's role to send their direct link.
  3. Prepare four original sentences. Finish each emphasis starter with your own idea. The clue guides the meaning; Grammar SOS shows a structural example from an unrelated context.
  4. Speak and react. Keep your sentences on screen. Use at least three naturally, tick them when spoken, and negotiate an ending.
  5. Swap roles or try another case. Your writing is saved on your device automatically, so you can return to it later.

Good to know: There is no timer, login or automatic grammar marking. The activity is designed for live pair speaking, with the teacher giving feedback. Notes are saved in this browser only and can be exported as text.
